## Customer VPN Design

| Customer | ASN | CEs | SPs | Service |
|----------|-----|-----|-----|---------|
| **Green** | 65910 | R9+R10 (AS65100), R18 (AS65200) | 65100 + 65200 | L3VPN eBGP |
| **Green (OSPF)** | — | R17 (AS65200) | 65200 | L3VPN OSPF PE-CE |
| **Yellow** | 65024 | R24 (AS65300), R26 (AS65200) | 65300 + 65200 | L3VPN eBGP |
| **Blue** | 65025 | R32 (AS65100), R25 (AS65200+65300) | 65100 + 65200 + 65300 | L3VPN eBGP (spans 3 ASes) |
| **Red VPLS** | — | R27 (R16), R28 (R14), R29 (R1) | 65100 + 65200 | VLAN 100 VPWS/VPLS (cross-AS) |

> **Note:** Customer Blue (R25) connects to 3 different ASes — this enables Inter-AS Options A/B/C testing. Red VPLS crosses AS boundaries — verify inter-AS L2VPN support on IOS 15.2. If unsupported, restrict VPLS to within AS 65200 (R27↔R28) and use AToM for cross-AS segments.
